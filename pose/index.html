2026-11-16 :: I love animated movie and content creations#

It is the only place where people enjoy all imperfections. Every animated movie (west) or Anime (asia) is mostly made for comedy and fun. But its suprising how people enjoy it very much and dont get into character shaming

Whereas contrast this in real life, people feel they have imperfect body and thanks to reels and spoiled by big media, we got impression that humans as in "we" must look this way for perfection or just to be seen good. Of course hitting gym is good and staying fit should be goal of all.

But what I mean is, why do we not accept imperfection for our human life but enjoy and appreciate on animated contents?

  • Every animated character has imperfect shape and bad proportion (some make good memes TBH)
  • We humans in-real-life care so much of good looking or ideals.
  • I hate that even some content creators are spoiled to create these images just to sell well or get more attraction.

2026-11-14 :: How I like/use and also hate AI#
  • AI is a great research companion
  • AI is a great tool to assist when brain is not working
  • AI can take the boring task and let us architect the task
  • AI is like any other crafting tool, you take the crafting art responsibility
  • A good prompt == best outcome, no blame to AI & its appreciated as a tool.
  • Only AI hatred I can feel genuinely is, if prompter does not know where the code got copied from, or from which learning it got results. Adding attributions actually help for more trust.
  • I hate people hating AI, just because of "why not". There are too many idiots who just proclaim that just because developer is using it they dismiss and fire it away. These people don't have work or things to get done TBH
  • I hate AI when it does anything more than I ask for, and goes beyong spitting out chunks of trash.
  • I hate people being divided over AI, middle ground is the only way. Both extremes are fools.
  • I hate when people think every AI user is a bad or ill-intentioned developer, these people go to extreme of not using it and even gaslighting anyone who mentions it (see mastodon threads for any)

I talk AI in the sense of Agent or assistant as tool only here.

2026-11-09 :: The comfort of borrowed choices.#

We have become so accustomed to what is served to us that we rarely pause to question what we actually need. From thoughts to food, entertainment to everyday choices, convenience has slowly replaced curiosity, and consumption has taken the place of creation. We mistake having endless options for having freedom, while rarely questioning who made those choices available to us in the first place.

Further reading

  • The Society of the Spectacle by Guy Debord
  • Amusing Ourselves to Death by Neil Postman
  • Manufacturing Consent by Edward S. Herman and Noam Chomsky
  • The Shallows by Nicholas Carr
