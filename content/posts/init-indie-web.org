As I've spoken on [[/posts/consumed-enough][prev post]], I accidentally discovered [[https://blr.indiewebclub.org/][Bengaluru Indie Web Club]] on mastodon by
abhinav tushar. And ever since I had a blog site, why did it not occur to me that I should have
searched for some clubs like these to improve my writing, and get motivated to write more. Maybe I
was too involved in [[/posts/may-i-recommend/][church of Emacs]], and as you see I started [[/posts/revamp-blog-2026][revamping this blog site]] from January,
and till July I did not get another push...?

Nevertheless, _every-time is a right time_ to start the work. So as of I'm writing this post, I've
attended my first Indie web meetup, and this is [[https://underline.center/t/indiewebclub-31][club's 31st meetup]]. So here's to the great day of
writers coming together, am writing this post based on the prompt of discovering a random image from
WikiMedia commons and making a post about it.

And look what I found randomly... Lucky for me it was a tree (garden or park Idk).

I dont know about the place since [[https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Palazzo_Boncompagni_-_Giornata_nazionale_delle_dimore_storiche_e_WP25BO_01.jpg][the page]] has all info in Italian language nor I'm writing about

today's session, so cheers to it.)

All I can see is the peaceful and lovely tree, sitting in the shadowy realms of every day life. Its
always the nature that makes me a take a moment in life, sit back, relax and reflect on whats
happening around me. Day by day life seems so busy that I fail to acknowledge whats even next to me,
so much focused work is also harming me lol.
And here's a simple park or a garden that can make me relive a lost moment and make me process my
living state and always guide me to think [[/posts/consumed-enough][what I've been doing and what's actually required]].

Apart from main focus on tree in the image, there are two people, a house behind, the one half of a
gate and so on, I'm not giving opinion on them, those are yours to interpret (if you can!) and
follow any details.. its a static image after all, I only wrote about =~160 words=, you fill the rest of the 840 words please!

Another things I learned today was that August month are considered as *Blaugust* time and Indie web
circle practice to publish one post each day, so totaling 31 posts for this month. Its easy to do micro
blog, but heck I need to find topics to even try it, but I may as well give it a try so I get
disciplined as well. This also stops me from updating my [[https://github.com/idlip/hugo-emacs-theme/][site eamcs theme]] and start writing more to utilize them feature-fully and then analyze the need and whats actually required for my site/posts.

- The 2026 edition is happening [[https://nerdybookahs.wordpress.com/2026/07/16/blaugust-2026-is-coming/][here]].

Finally, cheers to the first embark of long-overdue tasks on writing and to blog more. I tried to
start in Jan, and here finally in August 2026 I'm able to get the actual start. And on good side
I've been writing daily journal from past week, so that's again a good start to more writing.

Still I need to write more, but gosh Times Up! 30 mins got me only so far?
