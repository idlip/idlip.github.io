* [2026-11-26 Thu] When the hands know, the mind is free :habit:psychology:
My hands open reddit without asking me first. That is [[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muscle_memory][muscle memory]], and it never asked whether [[file:log/power-of-habit.org][the habit]] was worth learning. The same hands can learn to open the agenda, capture a note, or start the day's work before the mind has finished making excuses.

Muscle memory does not replace focus, it frees it. A driver who no longer thinks about the gears can watch the road. When the routine runs on its own, focus chimes in for the part that actually needs thinking, and the work starts taking care of itself. As [[https://www.thirdthinker.com/post/quote-i-fear-not-the-man-who-has-practiced-10000-kicks-once-brucelee][Bruce Lee]] is often quoted:

#+begin_quote
I fear not the man who has practiced 10,000 kicks once, but the man who has practiced one kick 10,000 times.
#+end_quote
  + Bruce Lee

So the real question is not how to stay focused, but what I am training my hands to do while I am not looking. Get into the [[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flow_(psychology)][flow]], not void.
