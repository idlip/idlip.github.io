Hey, there. Welcome to my little corner of the internet, where I write about Emacs, FOSS, and
whatever else I'm tinkering with.

This site holds a few [[/log/][blog posts]], written whenever something feels worth sharing.

Treasure this island has to offer: What I Log, pose bites, sites I wander to, what I'm watching and
reading, quotes I keep, and whatever I freshly bake with site.
