[[https://www.imdb.com/title/tt38504111/][Small Prophets]] is a new Apple TV series of 2026. Its more like a mini movie, which only has =6=
episodes and each runs for 30 mins.

Its a lively & a magical series which is so immersed in small view. You'll just see main character's
jungly house, some neighbors and a super mart store as work place. Just these and 3 unique and tiny
creatures called =Homunculi= which are unlike any other stuff I had seen before.

Its a fresh breather series with unique angle and peaceful take on future prediction and life.

I felt there was light british comedy here, but nothing that was funny. Just a subtle touch of
comedic side during work place conversation and incidents.

You might also want to read IMDB reviews since they also say good words on this series as I felt the
same. Heart warming, refreshing to watch and stirs your soul (maybe).

These kinda series or watch is absolutely required in these days, compared to more noise creating ones.

Edit: [2026-10-19]

After watching this, I wanted to watch the previous work by same writer. It was the [[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detectorists][Detectorists]]. Not the metal detectors, but Deterorists. LOL thats how they keep correcting everyone in the series.

Boy-o-boy, this was more funnier series and I watch all 3 seasons of it timely. Each season here is again, 6 episodes with 30mins runtime on each. I understood more satire and british comedy in this series and had a mild laugh over every 5-10 mins, thanks to Lance's intelligence.

TBH I'd rate Detectorist a solid =8.5= for me, and small prophets =8.0=. Both series happens in same place tbh, the house are same, but in detectorists MC are mostly on field trying to find gold (oh no, metals!). Even the OP short music is so soothing to hear and connects so well. Did not know guitar alone can sound so good; I reckon I had been listening to bad music then, gotta switch to good ones.

Even I'm happy to find detectorist work a good hobby and happy work to do, at least in the series it looked like it. Maybe it was the town and their livelihood that made me think so. Also every character that appears has funnier side, or just the conversations are funnier the other way. Terry's speech in DMDC meetings are always funnier and exciting to watch. This is a series where I did not hate nor find any reason for avoiding any character, every one had their meaning and good reason for watch time.
